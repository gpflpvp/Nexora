# Roadmap

The roadmap tracks public-facing development intent, not commitments or release dates.

## Planned foundation

- Android application shell and navigation
- Anime, movie, and TV catalog screens
- Media details, seasons, and episode selection
- Playback integration for supported media
- Search and discovery

## Deferred

- Watchlist
- Continue watching
- Profiles and accounts
- Offline downloads
- Casting
- Advanced playback and language controls

Deferred items will be reconsidered through the project's change process. Their inclusion here does not indicate that they are implemented.
